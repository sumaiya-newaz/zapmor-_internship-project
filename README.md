Zapmor

A modern and responsive appointment booking website built for Zapmor, a platform that connects customers with verified doctors and beauty professionals. The website lets users browse services, book appointments online, read customer testimonials, and get in touch with the team.

Features

🏠 Modern and responsive landing page
🩺 Doctor services (General Physician, Dentist, Dermatologist)
💇 Beauty parlor services (Haircut & Styling, Manicure & Pedicure, Facial & Spa)
🔀 Interactive Doctors / Beauty Parlors service toggle
📅 Online appointment booking form
🔗 Dynamic "Specific Service" dropdown based on the selected service type
💬 Customer testimonials
📊 Statistics section (appointments booked, verified providers, average rating)
📩 Contact form and contact information
📱 App download call-to-action (Zapmor User & Zapmor Partner)
✨ Floating animated cards, gradient buttons and smooth scrolling
📲 Mobile-friendly responsive design with auto-closing navbar menu

Main Sections

Home

Displays the hero section with the main call-to-action, platform statistics, and floating preview cards showing available slots and confirmed bookings.

Services

Shows the available services in two categories: Doctors and Beauty Parlors, with starting prices and a quick "Book now" link on each card.

Book Appointment

Allows customers to enter their name and email, choose a service type and a specific service, pick a preferred provider, select a date and time, and add extra notes.

Testimonials

Displays feedback from verified patients and customers.

About

Explains what Zapmor offers: instant confirmations, verified doctors and parlors, secure cashless payments, and 24/7 in-app support.

Contact

Provides a contact form, support email, and app download link.
